Destiny's Bounty is the airship and primary base of operations for the Ninja team. Originally a three-masted sailing vessel used by sky pirates before being commandeered, it was refitted by Zane and the team with four rear-mounted rotor-and-propeller engines that let it fly rather than merely sail the seas. The ship's main deck houses the helm, while below decks are the crew quarters, a shared kitchen, a workshop for equipment repair, and a brig used to hold captured enemies during transport. Sensei Wu's meditation quarters sit near the bow. The Bounty has been destroyed and rebuilt more than once over the team's history, each rebuild incorporating design changes the team learned from the previous ship's failures.

During the team's journeys through the Sea of Sand, the Bounty was fitted with a set of retractable treads and reinforced landing skids, converting it into a land-capable configuration sometimes referred to by the crew as the Land Bounty. In this configuration the rear propellers are angled to drive the tread assembly instead of providing lift, trading altitude for the ground clearance and traction needed to cross dune terrain that would ordinarily ground a flying vessel. The conversion is not permanent — reverting to standard flight configuration requires retracting the treads and re-angling the propeller assembly, a process the team can complete in the field with the ship's onboard tools.

Engineering notes on the Bounty's propulsion, as documented by Zane during a refit: four rear-mounted rotor engines, each independently throttled so the ship can yaw without a separate rudder; a lightweight hull frame prioritizing structural rigidity over raw armor thickness, since the ship relies on maneuverability rather than plating to avoid sustained damage; and a modular deck-mounted weapons rack that can be reconfigured between voyages depending on the mission — anything from simple cargo hooks to defensive ballistae. The treaded land configuration adds significant mass low in the hull, which the team found noticeably lowers the ship's center of gravity and improves stability on rough ground, at the cost of top speed compared to standard flight.

Zane Julien is the Nindroid Master of Ice. Unlike the other members of the team, Zane was built by his father, Dr. Julien, as an android — a fact Zane did not learn about himself until well into his time with the team, having believed himself human. Upon discovering his true nature, Zane worked through a period of identity crisis before coming to accept that being a Nindroid does not make him any less a person, nor any less a member of his family. He is formal, precise, and deeply literal in how he interprets language, a trait that stems directly from how he processes information, though his teammates have grown to find it endearing rather than off-putting.

Zane's elemental power is Ice, and as its Master he can generate and control ice and frost at will — freezing enemies in place, forming weapons and platforms out of solid ice, and enduring cold that would incapacitate anyone else on the team. His signature weapon is a pair of Shurikens of Ice. Later in his journey, Zane sacrificed himself to save his friends and the world from the Overlord, an act that led to his rebuilding as the Titanium Ninja — a more advanced, more resilient android form equipped with new augmentations while retaining the same core identity and memories.

The Ice Dragon is the elemental dragon spirit bound to Zane, called upon in moments of great need. Like the other Ninja's elemental dragons, it manifests as a creature made of Zane's own element — in his case, living ice and frost — and can carry him into battle or across terrain the Bounty cannot reach. Summoning it requires Zane to be in tune with his elemental power, something that comes more naturally to him the more he accepts his own nature as both machine and person.

Zane refers to the rest of the team — Kai, Jay, Cole, Lloyd, Nya, Sensei Wu, and P.I.X.A.L. — as his family, without qualification. P.I.X.A.L. in particular holds a distinct place for Zane: originally created by Dr. Julien's associate Cyrus Borg as an operating system before gaining true android sentience of her own, she and Zane share an understanding of what it means to be built rather than born, and to have had to decide for themselves what that does and doesn't change about who they are.
