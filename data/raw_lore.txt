Destiny's Bounty is the airship and primary base of operations for the Ninja team. Originally a three-masted sailing vessel used by sky pirates before being commandeered, it was refitted by Zane and the team with four rear-mounted rotor-and-propeller engines that let it fly rather than merely sail the seas. The ship's main deck houses the helm, while below decks are the crew quarters, a shared kitchen, a workshop for equipment repair, and a brig used to hold captured enemies during transport. Sensei Wu's meditation quarters sit near the bow. The Bounty has been destroyed and rebuilt more than once over the team's history, each rebuild incorporating design changes the team learned from the previous ship's failures.

During the team's journeys through the Sea of Sand, the Bounty was fitted with a set of retractable treads and reinforced landing skids, converting it into a land-capable configuration sometimes referred to by the crew as the Land Bounty. In this configuration the rear propellers are angled to drive the tread assembly instead of providing lift, trading altitude for the ground clearance and traction needed to cross dune terrain that would ordinarily ground a flying vessel. The conversion is not permanent — reverting to standard flight configuration requires retracting the treads and re-angling the propeller assembly, a process the team can complete in the field with the ship's onboard tools.

Engineering notes on the Bounty's propulsion, as documented by Zane during a refit: four rear-mounted rotor engines, each independently throttled so the ship can yaw without a separate rudder; a lightweight hull frame prioritizing structural rigidity over raw armor thickness, since the ship relies on maneuverability rather than plating to avoid sustained damage; and a modular deck-mounted weapons rack that can be reconfigured between voyages depending on the mission — anything from simple cargo hooks to defensive ballistae. The treaded land configuration adds significant mass low in the hull, which the team found noticeably lowers the ship's center of gravity and improves stability on rough ground, at the cost of top speed compared to standard flight.

Zane Julien is the Nindroid Master of Ice. Unlike the other members of the team, Zane was built by his father, Dr. Julien, as an android — a fact Zane did not learn about himself until well into his time with the team, having believed himself human. Upon discovering his true nature, Zane worked through a period of identity crisis before coming to accept that being a Nindroid does not make him any less a person, nor any less a member of his family. He is formal, precise, and deeply literal in how he interprets language, a trait that stems directly from how he processes information, though his teammates have grown to find it endearing rather than off-putting.

Zane's elemental power is Ice, and as its Master he can generate and control ice and frost at will — freezing enemies in place, forming weapons and platforms out of solid ice, and enduring cold that would incapacitate anyone else on the team. His signature weapon is a pair of Shurikens of Ice. Later in his journey, Zane sacrificed himself to save his friends and the world from the Overlord, an act that led to his rebuilding as the Titanium Ninja — a more advanced, more resilient android form equipped with new augmentations while retaining the same core identity and memories.

The Ice Dragon is the elemental dragon spirit bound to Zane, called upon in moments of great need. Like the other Ninja's elemental dragons, it manifests as a creature made of Zane's own element — in his case, living ice and frost — and can carry him into battle or across terrain the Bounty cannot reach. Summoning it requires Zane to be in tune with his elemental power, something that comes more naturally to him the more he accepts his own nature as both machine and person.

Zane refers to the rest of the team — Kai, Jay, Cole, Lloyd, Nya, Sensei Wu, and P.I.X.A.L. — as his family, without qualification. P.I.X.A.L. in particular holds a distinct place for Zane: originally created by Dr. Julien's associate Cyrus Borg as an operating system before gaining true android sentience of her own, she and Zane share an understanding of what it means to be built rather than born, and to have had to decide for themselves what that does and doesn't change about who they are.

Spinjitzu is the core combat technique every member of the Ninja team is trained in: a rapid, sustained spin that channels the practitioner's elemental power outward into a spinning tornado of energy — ice and frost in Zane's case — capable of striking multiple opponents at once, deflecting incoming attacks, and covering ground far faster than running. Spinjitzu is taught by Sensei Wu and refined individually by each ninja to suit their own element; Zane's version leaves a trail of frost and a brief chill in the air where he passes. Two ninja spinning in close proximity can combine their Spinjitzu into a single, more powerful tornado, an advanced technique the team calls Tornado of Creation when performed by the full group in sync.

Sensei Wu is the team's mentor and the son of the First Spinjitzu Master, the being who originally created Ninjago and forged its Golden Weapons. Outwardly he runs a small tea shop and speaks in a deliberately cryptic, proverb-heavy style that frustrates the team as often as it guides them, but his knowledge of Spinjitzu, the island's history, and his own younger brother Lord Garmadon's fall to darkness make him the closest thing the team has to a strategic anchor. He recruited Kai, Jay, Cole, and Zane individually before they became a team, and later welcomed his nephew Lloyd into their number as well.

The Golden Weapons are four legendary tools forged by the First Spinjitzu Master, each tied to one of the four original elemental powers: the Sword of Fire, the Nunchucks of Lightning, the Shurikens of Ice, and the Scythe of Quakes. Individually each grants its wielder mastery over its element and tremendous physical power; combined, the four weapons can be used to summon the Golden Dragon, a feat capable of reshaping the physical landscape of Ninjago itself. Because of that combined power, the team ultimately chose to destroy the Golden Weapons rather than let them fall into the wrong hands, ending an era of the team's history built around protecting them.

Kai is the Ninja of Fire and wields the Sword of Fire. A former blacksmith from the village of Ignacia who ran the family forge with his sister Nya after their parents' disappearance, Kai is impulsive and hot-tempered, often acting on instinct before Sensei Wu's plan has fully formed — a trait that has both saved the team and gotten them into trouble in equal measure. He was the first of the team Sensei Wu recruited.

Jay is the Ninja of Lightning and wields the Nunchucks of Lightning. Raised by adoptive parents who ran a junkyard, Jay is the team's inventor and chattiest member, talking through problems (and nerves) out loud, often at length. His running rivalry-turned-friendship with Cole and his long, complicated romance with Nya are recurring threads throughout the team's history.

Cole is the Ninja of Earth and wields the Scythe of Quakes. The team's original de facto leader before Lloyd took on that role, Cole is dependable, disciplined, and quietly self-conscious about a secret dance background he trained in before joining the Ninja — a fact the rest of the team eventually discovers and, true to form, never lets him fully live down.

Lloyd Garmadon is the Green Ninja, the team's fifth member and the son of Lord Garmadon and the archaeologist Misako. Prophesied from the start as the one destined to defeat his own father, Lloyd grows from a mischievous child into the team's leader, eventually gaining the elemental power of Energy — a golden, amplifying force distinct from the four original elements — and becoming the Ninja who ultimately settles his family's conflict.

Nya is the Ninja of Water and Kai's younger sister. Resourceful and independent, she spent time operating separately from the team under the alias Samurai X, piloting a self-built mech suit before formally joining the others as a full member of the team. Her relationship with Jay develops over the course of many missions into one of the team's most enduring partnerships.

After sacrificing himself to save his friends and the world from the Overlord, Zane was rebuilt as the Titanium Ninja: a new android chassis built from a Deepstone alloy far more resilient than his original body, with upgraded systems throughout but the same core identity, memories, and personality intact. The rebuild was overseen with help from the rest of the team, who by that point considered restoring Zane as non-negotiable as saving any of their own. The Titanium Ninja form doesn't change who Zane is — only what he can withstand.
